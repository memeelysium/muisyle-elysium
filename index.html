A CLEAR PATH FROM IDEA TO ON-CHAIN

How it works.

Five straightforward steps. You stay in control of your wallet and every transaction.

YOU CONTROL THE DEPLOYMENT
  1. 01

    Choose a network

    Select Elysium, Base or Robinhood Chain. Check the network name and make sure your wallet has the correct native gas token.

    NETWORK · GAS
  2. 02

    Configure your token

    Add a token name, ticker, logo and description. Review optional fees, burn allocation, project links and reward recipient.

    IDENTITY · SETTINGS
  3. 03

    Connect your wallet

    Connect a compatible EVM wallet and approve the requested network switch. Your wallet remains under your control.

    WALLET · PERMISSION
  4. 04

    Review and deploy

    Check the live preview, fee settings, reward address and risk notice. Submit, then review and sign the transaction in your wallet.

    FINAL REVIEW · SIGN
  5. 05

    Verify on-chain

    Wait for the transaction confirmation. Use the network explorer to inspect the token contract and transaction; eligible launches may appear in Launch History.

    CONFIRMATION · EXPLORER

Before you begin: Deployment requires network gas and the Clanker factory's launch fee. Token launches are irreversible, and trading, liquidity or token value are not guaranteed. Never share your recovery phrase or private key.
