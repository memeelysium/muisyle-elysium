TOKENOMICS · CLANKER V5

Burn and creator shares are calculated from the tax remaining after Clanker’s protocol fee, not from the full trade amount. The creator share is credited to the selected reward recipient.

Creator rewards will be sent to this address. Leave blank to use the deployer wallet. Make sure the address owner has agreed to receive them.

You may add the project's X account or another account. The X link is profile metadata only; fee rewards are still sent to the recipient wallet above.

Your connected wallet remains the deployer and creator admin. Creator rewards go to the reward recipient you select; if left blank, they go to your connected wallet. MUISYLE never receives or stores your private key.

Important trading-risk notice: Clanker V5 applies the selected buy and sell tax to trades. During the first 20 seconds, anti-snipe protection can raise the total fee to as much as 99%, then it decreases to the selected tax. Burn allocation is calculated from the tax remaining after Clanker’s protocol fee. These settings are fixed at launch, and transactions cannot be casually reversed. Token value, liquidity and trading activity are not guaranteed.

A CLEAR PATH FROM IDEA TO ON-CHAIN

How it works.

Five straightforward steps. You stay in control of your wallet and every transaction.

YOU CONTROL THE DEPLOYMENT
  1. 01

    Choose a network

    Select Elysium, Base or Robinhood Chain. Check the network name and make sure your wallet has the correct native gas token.

    NETWORK · GAS
  2. 02

    Configure your token

    Add a token name, ticker, logo and description. Review optional fees, burn allocation, project links and reward recipient.

    IDENTITY · SETTINGS
  3. 03

    Connect your wallet

    Connect a compatible EVM wallet and approve the requested network switch. Your wallet remains under your control.

    WALLET · PERMISSION
  4. 04

    Review and deploy

    Check the live preview, fee settings, reward address and risk notice. Submit, then review and sign the transaction in your wallet.

    FINAL REVIEW · SIGN
  5. 05

    Verify on-chain

    Wait for the transaction confirmation. Use the network explorer to inspect the token contract and transaction; eligible launches may appear in Launch History.

    CONFIRMATION · EXPLORER

Before you begin: Deployment requires network gas and the Clanker factory's launch fee. Token launches are irreversible, and trading, liquidity or token value are not guaranteed. Never share your recovery phrase or private key.

03 / CREATOR REWARDS

CLAIM YOUR
FEES.

Check and claim eligible Clanker creator rewards. Enter the token contract address to open its official Clanker Admin page.

CLANKER REWARDS

Use the token contract address, not your wallet address. Connect the reward recipient wallet on Clanker and review the claim before signing.

Claims are processed on Clanker's official website.

MUISYLE does not custody rewards or request private keys. The official Clanker page displays available rewards and asks you to approve any claim transaction in your wallet.

Clanker v5 rewards are accumulated in FeeEscrow. Older Clanker versions use their respective reward contracts. Availability and supported networks depend on the token's launch version.
